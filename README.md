#Project tittle: Physiotherapy System

#1.1 Introduction
The subject of this document is the gym physiotherapy system. This chapter provides an overview of the proposed solution, starting with a summary of its core purpose and concluding with a detailed description of the system's objectives and intended features. 
In today's fast-paced world, the importance of physical and mental well-being has never been more recognized. While fitness centres cater to individuals aiming to stay fit, they often lack the resources to support people with specific physical limitations and those undergoing rehabilitation. On the other hand, physiotherapy services are often standalone and disconnected from modern fitness environments. This disconnect can hinder recovery, progress, and long-term health outcomes.
The gym physiotherapy system is a hybrid solution that integrates professional physiotherapy with personalized fitness training. The system is designed to be inclusive and adaptive catering to both rehabilitation needs and fitness goals.


#1.2 Executive Summary
The gym physiotherapy system is an innovative and comprehensive platform that merges physiotherapy and fitness into a unified, user-friendly digital environment. It is designed to create a therapeutic and supportive fitness ecosystem for users of all capabilities.
The system focuses on delivering personalized exercise routines under professional guidance, enabling users to participate in safe, effective physical activity whether recovering from an injury, managing chronic conditions, or pursuing fitness objectives. By incorporating features such as online physiotherapy sessions, adaptive workout planning, goal setting, and progress tracking, the platform aims to enhance both physical and mental well-being. It also supports secure online consultations.

#1.3 Description of Existing System
Traditional gym and fitness platforms generally cater to healthy individuals and often lack features tailored to those with physical limitations and special rehabilitation needs. These systems typically do not include personalized therapeutic exercise programs, professional physiotherapy guidance, limiting their accessibility and effectiveness for individuals with different health conditions or varying fitness levels.

#Further limitations of current systems include:
•	Lack of Specialized Adaptation: Most platforms offer generic workout libraries that assume a baseline level of physical health. They cannot dynamically adapt routines for users with chronic pain, post-operative recovery needs, or specific musculoskeletal conditions, which can lead to injury or ineffective training.
•	Absence of Professional Oversight: Existing platforms do not offer integrated features for licensed physiotherapists to remotely review form, provide real-time feedback, or make professional adjustments to a therapeutic exercise regimen. This creates a significant risk gap for users needing expert guidance.


#1.4 Scope
The scope of the gym fitness physiotherapy system includes the development of a hybrid platform that combines physiotherapy support with fitness training. It encompasses progress monitoring, adaptive planning, and setting goals, making it suitable for a wide range of users including those with special fitness needs. It allows for online scheduling. The system will also support online consultations and analytical insights for better decision-making and care management.

#In-Scope Deliverables:
	 Web Application Development (Client/Clinician Interfaces): Creation of highly responsive, intuitive interfaces to capture and input baseline user capacity and subsequent session-specific subjective workload ratings ( pain scores).
	Data Analytics and Reporting: Advanced dashboards and reporting tools for physiotherapists to analyze patient adherence rates, performance trends (e.g., strength gains, mobility improvement), key rehabilitation metrics, and overall population health statistics, enabling data-driven care adjustments and ongoing assessment of user workload.
	Integrated Monitoring and Feedback: Web functionality for users to log completed exercises, rate perceived exertion (RPE), pain levels, and any functional changes immediately after a session.
	Personalized Plan Engine: A sophisticated back-end system utilizing rule-based logic to dynamically generate workout/rehabilitation plans. This engine must ingest real-time user-logged data and physiotherapist input to suggest safe, progressive, and personalized plan modifications that align with user-set goals.
	Goal Setting Framework: Dedicated user interface where users can define, monitor, and adjust specific fitness and therapy goals
Out-of-Scope Activities:
	Integration with external electronic health record (EHR) systems.
	Direct processing of insurance claims (the system will provide necessary documentation for user reimbursement but will not handle the claims submission process itself).
	Sale, rental, or maintenance of physical fitness or physiotherapy equipment.


#1.5 Objectives
The objectives of the system are:
	To access Assess the workload
	Track fitness progress
	 Create a workout plan

#1.6 Description of Proposed System
The proposed gym fitness physiotherapy system will offer an integrated platform that combines fitness training with physiotherapy services. Key features include online consultation booking  and personalized workout routines supported by physiotherapists. Users will be able to log workouts, monitor their progress, set fitness goals, and access fitness-related analysis. The system is designed to support physical improvement, boost mental well-being, and encourage social inclusion, ultimately enhancing users' independence and overall health.

#Key Functional Components:
•	User/Client Portal: Provides a personalized dashboard showing the current workout/rehabilitation plan, progress summaries (using both fitness and rehabilitation metrics), and a secure messaging tool to communicate with their assigned physiotherapist.
•	Physiotherapist/Clinician Portal: A comprehensive workspace enabling clinicians to manage their patient load, create custom therapeutic exercise libraries, dynamically adjust patient workout variables (sets, reps, rest time, resistance) based on logged progress, and conduct virtual consultations.
•	Integrated Monitoring and Feedback: The system will prompt users for crucial subjective data (like pain levels) after each session, which is immediately visible to the clinician, allowing for timely intervention and plan adjustment.
•	Financial and Administrative Module: Handles the secure online payment workflow for sessions and manages user subscription status.
